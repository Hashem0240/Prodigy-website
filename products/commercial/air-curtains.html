D & Q SERIES

Air Curtains

A continuous air barrier above open doorways, helping reduce indoor and outdoor air exchange. Two profiles for commercial entrances.

Download data sheet (PDF)

D Series

Curved profile · widths 900–2000 mm

View models

Q Series

Contemporary profile · widths 600–2000 mm

View models

Operation & installation

Two speeds

High / low air velocity: 11 / 9 m/s. Supply: 220–240 V, 50/60 Hz.

Indoor doorway mounting

Recommended mounting height: 2.5–3.0 m. Select the width to cover the entrance.

Control options

Manual or remote control, with an optional door sensor. Availability depends on configuration.
