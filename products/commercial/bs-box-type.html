CABINET FANS / BS SERIES

Box-Type Inline Duct Fans

Two-speed duct ventilation in a metal box casing, with circular inlet and outlet connections for residential and commercial systems.

  • Nominal duct sizes: 100–300 mm
  • High-speed airflow ratings: 120–4000 m³/h
  • Performance ratings: 220 V / 50 Hz
Download data sheet (PDF)

Construction & features

Metal casing

Powder-coated casing with a removable cover for maintenance.

Two-speed motor

Copper motor with ball bearings and high / low operation.

Circular connections

Round duct connections with an integrated butterfly backdraft damper.
